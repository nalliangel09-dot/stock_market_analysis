### **Project Title: Multi-Asset Financial Performance & Risk Analysis Pipeline**

#### **Executive Summary**

This project involves the development of an end-to-end Jupyter notebook-based analytics pipeline designed to evaluate the 5-year historical performance, risk profiles, and cumulative returns of major technology equities (AAPLe, AMAZON, GOOGLE, MICROSOFT). The objective is to identify top-performing assets and simulate investment strategies based on quantitative metrics.

#### **Key Workflow & Methodologies**

* **Data Collection & Extraction:** Automated retrieval of 5-year historical stock price data using robust financial data APIs.
* **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Handling missing values, adjusting for corporate actions (splits/dividends), and aligning timestamps across multi-ticker datasets.
* **Performance & Risk Analysis:**
* Computation of cumulative returns to evaluate long-term capital appreciation.
* Assessment of volatility and risk metrics (e.g., standard deviation, Sharpe ratio) to gauge risk-adjusted performance.


* **Investment Simulation:** Modeling historical portfolio allocation and simulating growth trajectories to benchmark asset performance.

#### **Tech Stack & Libraries**

* **Language:** Python
* **Data Manipulation & Analysis:** Pandas, NumPy
* **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
* **Environment:** Jupyter Notebook
